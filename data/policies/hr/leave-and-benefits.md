# Leave and Benefits Policy (HR-POL-102)

## Annual leave
- 20 days paid annual leave per year (pro-rated for new hires).
- Carry-over: maximum 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Sick leave
- 10 days paid sick leave per year.
- Medical certificate required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
- 16 weeks primary caregiver leave at full pay.
- 4 weeks secondary caregiver leave at full pay.

## Health benefits
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage from day one.
- Mental health support: 8 counseling sessions per year via EAP.

## Appraisals
All employees participate in a mid-year check-in and an annual appraisal cycle. See the Appraisal Process policy.
