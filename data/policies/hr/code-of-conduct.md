# Code of Conduct (HR-POL-104)

## Standards
All employees must act with integrity, respect colleagues, and protect company confidential information.

## Conflicts of interest
Disclose any outside employment or financial interest that may conflict with company duties.

## Harassment
Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Report concerns to HR or the ethics hotline.

## Data protection
Customer and employee data must be handled per the Information Security policy. Do not share credentials.
