# Remote Work Policy (HR-POL-101)

Effective: 2024-01-01

## Eligibility
Employees in Engineering, Product, and HR may work remotely up to 3 days per week after 90 days of employment.

## Approval
Managers must approve remote schedules in writing each quarter. Requests are recorded in the HR portal.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop stipend of $500 annually for home office setup.

## Performance
Remote employees are evaluated on the same appraisal criteria as in-office staff.
