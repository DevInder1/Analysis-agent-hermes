# Performance Appraisal Process (HR-POL-103)

## Cycle
- **Mid-year check-in:** June — goals progress only, no rating.
- **Annual appraisal:** December — full rating and compensation review input.

## Stages
1. **Self-assessment** — Employee submits goals, accomplishments, and development requests (due: 1 Dec).
2. **Manager review** — Manager rates performance 1–5 and provides written feedback (due: 15 Dec).
3. **Calibration** — Leadership reviews ratings by department (due: 22 Dec).
4. **Delivery** — Manager shares final rating with employee (due: 31 Dec).

## Rating scale
| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 5 | Exceptional — consistently exceeds expectations |
| 4 | Strong — exceeds expectations in key areas |
| 3 | Meets — delivers expected outcomes |
| 2 | Developing — improvement needed |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory — formal plan required |

## Tools
Use MCP tools `get_appraisal_status`, `submit_appraisal_self`, and `submit_manager_review` to track and update records in the company system.

## Appeals
Employees may request a review of their rating within 10 business days of delivery.
