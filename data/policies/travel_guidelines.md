# Corporate Travel Guidelines (FIN-TRAVEL-001)

## Pre-approval
All travel over $500 requires manager approval before booking. International travel requires director approval.

## Booking
- Use the corporate travel portal for flights and hotels.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class permitted for flights over 6 hours with director approval.

## Daily allowances (per diem)
| Region | Meals | Incidentals |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Domestic | $75 | $25 |
| Europe | $90 | $30 |
| Asia-Pacific | $85 | $30 |

## Expense reporting
- Submit expenses within 14 days of return.
- Receipts required for any single expense over $25.
- Project travel must be charged to the correct project cost center.

## Budget alignment
Travel spend is tracked against project `travel_expense` budgets. Projects at **At Risk** status require VP Finance approval for new trips.

## Sustainability
Prefer rail over air for trips under 4 hours where service is available.
